# Examine a system

**Pick when:** the goal is a quality, not a change: can we improve, where are we weak, why is it unreliable or slow.
**Chain:** investigate, assess
**Done looks like:** findings ranked with evidence, and a verdict on the top candidate fix.

## Steps
1. **investigate**. Hand it the goal as a symptom. Out: ranked findings marked proven or claimed, with candidate fixes.
2. **assess** on the top candidate, or on the top two or three as options. Out: the verdict.
3. Do → continue with change-code for that candidate, one unit per fix. A candidate that adds a slice, moves a seam, or needs a new cross-slice edge is a map change, so it goes to **architect** in Discuss mode instead, or into the report as a proposed map change when the user is away.

## Watch for
- Fixing while examining. Examination builds nothing.
- A single concrete failure is fix-bug, not this playbook.
